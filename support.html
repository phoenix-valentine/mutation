Mutation Support

Mutation: Become the Monster, for iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web.

Get help or report a bug

  • In the game: open the pause menu or the title screen and press Feedback. It can include game details that help fix the problem.
  • By email: mutationbecomethemonster@gmail.com

Common questions

How do I play? Move with one thumb (or WASD, arrows or a gamepad). Your monster attacks on its own. Eat humans, collect flesh, pick a mutation at each level-up and survive four acts and their bosses.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. An unfinished hunt can be continued from the title screen. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

The game runs slowly. Open Settings and set Graphics to Low.

Full screen on Mac. Press F in a menu or on the pause screen, use Settings > Full screen, or the green window button.
