# Mutation: GOOD file

**Core sentence:** You eat humans to grow a monster body, one visible mutation at a time, while humanity escalates from villagers to an army to stop you.

**Rule:** before adding anything, say how it serves the core sentence, what it costs in clarity, and what could be removed instead.

## Budget

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Mutations | 47 (36 organs and traits, 6 trophies, 5 Apex Genes) |
| Hybrids | 14 |
| Enemy types on screen at once | about 4 to 6 |
| Enemy types per act roster | Act I: 6, Act II: 8, Act III: 9 plus 3 vehicles, Finale: 5 plus the walker |
| Elite modifiers | 4 |
| HUD during play | Health and XP, the threat bar, Biomass, Pause, Dash and Apex. Chips appear only when they ask for an action |
| Settings | 19 |

## The simplification pass (v1-full → v2)

### Cut

**Systems**
- Encounters (5)
- Contracts
- Flesh Pods
- Call the boss
- Doctrines, including the pause tab, the Molting reveal and the HUD icon
- Strains (7)
- Tissues (12)
- Mutators
- Organism Mastery
- Endless
- Lock
- The paid "+1 card" button

**Mutations (16)**
- Parasite Tongue
- Orbiting Teeth
- Siege Organ (merged into Explosive Sac)
- Spore Mortar
- Toxic Spines
- Leech Tail
- Corrosive Blood
- Hardened Organs
- Adaptive Skin
- Egg Sacs
- Second Stomach
- Fungal Network
- Thrall Spores
- Combustible Organs
- Necrotic Tissue
- Expanded Neural Tissue

**Other content**
- Hybrids: Chain Reaction and Corrosive Ecosystem.
- Elite modifiers: Tactical and Medic-Supported.
- Squad rosters:
  - Act II: searchlight, tracker, breacher, tac marksman, K9.
  - Act III: researcher, demo engineer, hazmat, drone operator, EW specialist, AT specialist, the tactical vehicle and the heavy drone.
  - Finale: AT and EW specialists, the heavy drone.
- Act III events: Sniper Nest and Helicopter Flyover.
- Deeds that only gave titles: no dash, 50 perfect dashes, starved, feast.
- The TOXIN 6 deed.
- Settings: Fewer repeat sounds (always on now), Effect opacity, Fill wide screens.
- Sounds of the cut organs.

### Merged

- Nest packs: 7 → 3. DNA for the removed packs, and for Lock, is refunded on load.
- Short and Sprint toggles → one Length control (Full / Short / Sprint).
- Siege Organ → Explosive Sac. Acid Artillery and the Artillery form now key off the Sac.

### Quieted

**Damage numbers**
- They show only on bosses, elites and vehicles, with no poison or bleed ticks.
- At most 12 at once; only crits pop big.

**Floating text**
- No heal, Biomass, PERFECT or ENCOUNTER popups.
- No "!" over every human.
- Health bars show only over elites.

**HUD**
- The organ strip moved to the pause screen.
- No mode, slow, lit, tracked, frenzy, form-progress or contract chips.
- No strobing edge bars.
- Banners are one small line under the threat bar and last 1.8 s.

**Gore and particles**
- About half the particles.
- Blood stains fade in about 7 s.
- XP orbs merge past 60 on screen and are smaller with a softer glow.

**Crowd**
- Late-act density is lower: 1.1× instead of 1.22×.

### Re-tuned

- +1 max health per level, which replaces the health from Tissues and Holdouts.

## Second pass: menus, settings and text

- **Main menu:** it grows with you. A first-time player sees Hunt, Settings and Feedback. Choose organism, Daily, Journal and Nest appear after the first hunt; Boss Rehearsal appears after the first boss.
  - One Hunt button starts straight away with your organism.
  - Full screen left the menu (it is in Settings, and on the F key).
  - Last hunt appears only once there is one.
- **Title:** the sound status line became one "Headphones recommended" hint on desktop.
- **Organism screen:**
  - The Bounty panel shows only after it unlocks (win a run).
  - The Seed button is gone.
- **Settings: 19 → 18, and each device shows only what it can use.**
  - Desktop has no touch or vibration options.
  - Vibration shows only where the browser has it; Full screen only where it works.
  - Copy and Paste save code are now one Save code sheet.
  - The sound status line is gone.
- **Nest tabs:** Choices and Card pool became one Upgrades tab, so there are 3 tabs.
- **Journal:** the Deeds tab is gone; deeds live in the Nest next to what they unlock.
- **Pause, Stats tab:** only the stats your build has changed, plus health and speed, instead of 14 rows of base values.
- **Level-up and Chrysalis:** the empty "Tap a card to see..." box is gone; the subtitle already says it.
- **Feedback:** categories 9 → 6 (Bug, Balance, Idea, Confusing, Praise, Other).

## IDEAS (parked, not in the game)

- Encounters (Holdout, Slaughterhouse) could come back as a rare event that uses the existing event banner, if runs feel samey.
- Strains as a Bounty modifier rather than a card.
- Mastery as a cosmetic-only frame.
- Endless as a separate menu mode, if players ask for it.
