Privacy Policy

Mutation: Become the Monster. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Mutation is a game you play on your own device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocks, DNA, settings, records, a saved hunt) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage (or your browser's storage on the web version). It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is sent, and only when you choose to

The in-game Feedback form is the only thing that sends data over the internet. When you press Send, it sends:

  • the message you typed and the category you picked;
  • a name and an email address, only if you typed them in (both are optional);
  • if you leave "Include game details" switched on: the organism, level, act, hunt time, game mode and any recent error messages, to help fix bugs. You can switch this off before sending.

The message is delivered to the developer's email inbox through the form service Web3Forms (or FormSubmit as a fallback), which only passes it on. It is used only to read your feedback, fix problems and, if you left an email, reply to you. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Feedback form does not require a name or email.

Deleting your data

To remove a feedback message you sent, write to the address below and it will be deleted.
